Software built for how Sierra Leone actually works

TechLedger Solutions designs and builds the systems institutions use to run their day-to-day operations — records and registers, applications and approvals, field reporting, payments, and the dashboards leadership reads to make decisions.

We build for the conditions we work in. Connectivity is variable, staff are spread across districts, most users are on a phone, and reporting deadlines do not move. Those are design constraints from the first day of a project, not problems patched in afterwards.

We were established in 2024 and now run platforms for the Ministry of Health and Sanitation, the College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences, the Sierra Leone Paediatric and Neonatal Nurses Association, and private sector clients.

How we work

Four stages, each ending in something you can see and sign off on.

  1. Discovery We audit your current workflow, data quality and constraints before designing anything. The output is a written scope both sides agree to.
  2. Design and prototype You review a working prototype before full development begins, so there are no surprises about what is being built.
  3. Build and test Delivery in clear milestones with testing at each stage. Progress is reported against the agreed schedule.
  4. Deploy and support We deploy, train your team, hand over documentation, and stay reachable. You own the code, the data and the platform.

Why organisations choose us

  • Local context, global standards. We design for variable connectivity, mobile-first users and local payment systems — to international engineering quality.
  • We finish what we start. Realistic timelines, clear communication, and we remain reachable after launch rather than disappearing at handover.
  • You own everything. Full ownership of your code, your data and your platform. No lock-in, no hidden licence fees, and documentation another team could pick up.
  • One accountable team. Engineering, data and financial analysis sit together, so a problem goes from diagnosis to a running system without being handed around.
