Platform Features

Everything a College Needs to Run Academically

From admissions to graduation — every stage of the student journey is managed in one connected system.

Student Enrollment

Online application and enrollment system. Students register, upload documents, get assigned student IDs and course schedules automatically.

Grade Management

Lecturers enter continuous assessments and exam scores. The system calculates final grades, GPA, and generates transcripts automatically.

Attendance Tracking

Lecturers mark daily attendance per class. Automated alerts are sent to students and parents when attendance falls below the required threshold.

Fee Management

Track tuition fees, hostel charges, and exam fees per student. Generate payment receipts, flag outstanding balances, and produce financial summaries.

Timetable & Scheduling

Build and publish semester timetables for all departments. Room allocation, lecturer assignments, and clash detection built in.

Reports & Transcripts

Auto-generate official transcripts, result slips, and end-of-year reports as downloadable PDFs — reducing the admin burden on staff significantly.

The Challenge

Registers, spreadsheets, and manual everything

Before TechLedger, the college relied on paper registers, Excel spreadsheets, and manual processes that created delays, errors, and frustrated both staff and students.

  • Grade results took weeks to compile and announce
  • Fee payment tracking was inaccurate and hard to audit
  • Attendance was marked on paper — impossible to analyse
  • Producing a single transcript took days of admin work
  • No central view of how the institution was performing
Our Solution

One system. Every process. Zero paper.

  • Student records, grades, attendance — all in one database
  • Automated grade calculation and transcript generation
  • Fee ledgers with payment history and outstanding balance alerts
  • Lecturer and admin portals with role-based access
  • Management dashboard with real-time institutional analytics
