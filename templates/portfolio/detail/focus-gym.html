Platform Features

Everything a Gym Needs to Run Efficiently

From the front desk to the back office — all in one place, built for Sierra Leone's fitness industry.

Member Management

Register new members, manage profiles, track membership plans, and view full history — all searchable in seconds.

Attendance Tracking

Digital check-in system records every gym visit with timestamps — giving staff real-time visibility of who's in the gym.

Payments & Billing

Integrated Monime mobile money payments. Members pay via phone, receipts are auto-generated, and overdue accounts are flagged automatically.

Automated Reminders

Members receive automated SMS/email reminders before their membership expires — reducing churn and manual follow-up work for staff.

Analytics & Reports

Revenue reports, membership growth charts, peak-hour attendance heatmaps — giving gym owners real data to make business decisions.

Staff Dashboard

Role-based access for gym managers and front-desk staff. Each role sees only what they need — keeping operations clean and secure.

The Challenge

Paper registers were costing them money

Before TechLedger, Focus Gym was managing 400+ members using paper records, WhatsApp messages, and manual bank transfers. This created:

  • Members slipping through without paying renewals
  • No visibility on who was actually in the gym day-to-day
  • Hours spent each month manually compiling payment records
  • No data to make decisions about peak times, pricing, or staffing
  • A front-desk process that frustrated both staff and members
Our Solution

A single platform that runs the whole gym

  • Full member database with subscription tracking and auto-renewal alerts
  • Digital check-in at the front desk — one tap, instant record
  • Monime mobile money integration for frictionless SLE payments
  • Owner dashboard with revenue, attendance, and membership analytics
  • Deployed on cloud with 99.9% uptime — always accessible
