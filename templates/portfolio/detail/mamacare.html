Platform Features

Designed for Sierra Leone's Mothers

Simple enough for first-time smartphone users. Powerful enough for clinicians and health workers to coordinate care across districts.

Pregnancy Tracking

Week-by-week pregnancy updates, baby development milestones, and personalised health tips sent directly to each mother via SMS and app.

Appointment Booking

Mothers book antenatal clinic visits online. Automated SMS reminders reduce missed appointments and ensure timely antenatal care.

SMS in Krio & English

Health reminders and alerts are sent in both English and Krio — Sierra Leone's lingua franca — making the platform accessible to all mothers regardless of literacy level.

High-Risk Flagging

The system automatically identifies high-risk pregnancies based on reported symptoms and vital signs — alerting midwives and health workers to prioritise these cases.

Community Health Workers

CHWs get their own mobile interface to register and monitor mothers in their communities — syncing when connectivity is available.

District Health Analytics

District health officers see real-time data on maternal registrations, appointment attendance, high-risk cases, and delivery outcomes by district.

The Challenge

Sierra Leone's maternal mortality is among the highest globally

Many preventable maternal deaths in Sierra Leone stem from missed antenatal visits, late detection of complications, and poor communication between health workers and mothers.

  • Mothers in rural areas missing critical antenatal appointments
  • No system to identify and prioritise high-risk pregnancies
  • Health workers unable to track mothers between visits
  • Language barriers preventing mothers from understanding health advice
  • Paper-based records making coordination between clinics impossible
Our Solution

Technology in service of every mother

  • SMS reminders in Krio and English ensure no mother misses an appointment
  • Automated high-risk pregnancy detection alerts health workers immediately
  • Community health workers can register and track mothers offline
  • Digital records shared between clinics — mothers are never lost in the system
  • District dashboards give health officers real-time visibility across their area
