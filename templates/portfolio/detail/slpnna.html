Platform Features

Built for a National Police Association

A secure, centralised platform that gives SLPNNA leadership full visibility over their membership, communications, and welfare programmes.

Officer Directory

Searchable national database of all registered officers — filterable by rank, division, station, and service date.

Communications Hub

Send announcements, circulars, and alerts to all members or targeted divisions — with email delivery and read receipts.

Events Management

Schedule and manage association events — meetings, training, welfare gatherings — with RSVP tracking and attendance records.

Welfare Tracking

Record and manage welfare cases, benefit claims, and support requests — ensuring no officer's case falls through the cracks.

Role-Based Access

Separate portals for national leadership, divisional commanders, station officers, and regular members — each with appropriate permissions.

Reports & Analytics

Leadership dashboards showing membership statistics, division breakdowns, event participation rates, and welfare programme metrics.

The Challenge

A national association running on paper and WhatsApp

SLPNNA had no centralised system. Officers were managed via spreadsheets, communication happened informally, and there was no reliable way to track welfare cases or event attendance across divisions.

  • No single source of truth for member records
  • Announcements reached some divisions but not others
  • Welfare cases were tracked informally and often lost
  • Leadership had no data on membership trends or participation
  • New officer registration was slow and paper-based
Our Solution

One platform for the entire national network

  • Central officer database — searchable by name, rank, division, or badge number
  • Broadcast messaging system with email delivery to all or targeted groups
  • Welfare case management with status tracking and resolution logging
  • Leadership analytics dashboard with exportable reports
  • Secure, role-based access — right information to the right people
