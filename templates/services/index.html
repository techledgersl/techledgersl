Technology and standards

  • Python and Django. A mature, secure framework used by government and enterprise systems worldwide — not a niche stack that leaves you dependent on one supplier.
  • PostgreSQL. A proven relational database, suitable for records that have to stand up to audit.
  • Offline-capable field tools. Data captured without connectivity and synchronised automatically when the network returns.
  • Role-based access control. Every user sees only what their role permits, with audit trails on the records that matter.
  • Mobile money integration. Live payment collection, automatic receipting and reconciled revenue reporting, already running in production.
  • Full client ownership. You own the code, the data and the platform. No lock-in, and documentation another team could pick up.

How an engagement runs

  1. Discovery We learn your users, constraints and operating environment before designing a single screen.
  2. Design and prototype You review a working prototype before full development begins.
  3. Build and test Clean, maintainable code tested at every stage, delivered in milestones.
  4. Deploy and support We deploy, train your team, and stay reachable after launch.
